- Chrome web-app windows with unavailable or blank titles now show the application's name in switchers and tooltips. Window restoration preserves the distinction between unavailable and empty titles.
- Spanning a window at a display edge now shrinks the span by one section. A span reduced to one section returns to that section; spanning stays on the same display.
- During section focus, other occupied sections retain empty, half-height switcher capsules in their existing positions. Clicking a capsule switches to that section and leaves focus mode.
- Updated source, feedback, and Homebrew download links to the renamed `RUverse/panoptos-mac` repository.
